Photos of Earth from a million miles away

NASA's EPIC camera rides on the DSCOVR spacecraft, parked between Earth and the Sun. It always looks at Earth's sunny side and takes photos all day long. Take your own "photos" and compare them.

Check it yourself with the real photos

  1. Open NASA's EPIC website: epic.gsfc.nasa.gov. It shows a set of real photos from one recent day.
  2. Step through the photos from first to last. Watch one continent, like Africa or the Americas, slide across from one side and off the other.
  3. Notice what stays the same: the camera always sees a fully sunlit Earth. The sunny side keeps facing the Sun while the land turns through it.
  4. To see a whole day at once, watch NASA's animation "As the World Turns", made from one day of EPIC photos.
